Flipback — Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Flipback is a reverse singing game for Android, published by ikistudio.

What we collect

We collect nothing. Flipback has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. Your recordings are processed on your phone and are never uploaded by the app.

The only part of the app that uses the internet is the Google Play Billing library, which Google requires for purchases. It sends the state of your purchase (purchase history) to Google Play so the app knows whether your Party Pass or yearly plan is active, and it may send Google technical diagnostics about that connection. That data goes to Google, not to us, and we do not sell or share it.

Microphone and recordings

Flipback uses the microphone only while you hold the record button during a round, for up to 10 seconds per take. It always uses the phone's own microphone. Recordings, the reversed versions and the scores are computed and stored on your phone, in the app's private storage. The app keeps your last 30 rounds; older ones are deleted automatically.

You can delete one round in Recent rounds, or all recordings in Settings → "Delete all recordings". Uninstalling the app deletes them too. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your own backup; Google runs that backup and you control it in Android settings. We have no access to it.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you tap Share. Then Flipback makes one audio file (the original line and your take, flipped back) and hands it to the Android share sheet; you choose the app it goes to, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Microphone — to record your takes during a round (see above).
  • Internet, network state and Google Play billing — used only by the Google Play Billing library for purchases.

Children

Flipback is not directed at children under 13. It shows no ads, has no account and sends no recordings anywhere.
