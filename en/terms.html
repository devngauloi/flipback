Flipback — Terms of use

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The app

Flipback is a reverse singing game: record a line, hear it backwards, sing it backwards and get a score from 0 to 100. The score is a game estimate computed on your phone, not a judgement of your voice.

Free rounds and purchases

  • 3 full rounds are free, with no ads. Recording a line and hearing it backwards stay free after that.
  • Party Pass: one payment for 7 days of unlimited rounds. It does not renew.
  • Year: one payment per year. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Prices are shown in the app and in Google Play before you confirm, and may differ by country. Payment is taken by your Google Play account.
  • Cancel the yearly plan any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from Flipback → Settings → "Manage in Google Play".
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Your recordings

Your recordings stay on your phone (see the privacy policy). When you share a take, you decide where it goes. Only record and share voices of people who agree to it.

No warranty

The app is provided as is. Scores depend on the phone, the room and the microphone.
